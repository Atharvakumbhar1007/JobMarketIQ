# Business Questions Answered by JobMarketIQ

This document outlines the core business and analytical questions that the JobMarketIQ platform is designed to answer.

## 1. Skill Demand & Market Trends
- What are the top 20 most demanded technical skills across all job postings?
- How does skill demand vary by category (e.g., Languages vs. Frameworks vs. Cloud)?
- Which skills are most frequently requested together (co-occurrence)?
- What are the defining skills for specific roles (e.g., Data Scientist vs. Frontend Developer)?

## 2. Salary Intelligence
- What is the average, median, and range of salaries for different job titles?
- How does compensation scale with years of experience?
- Is there a salary premium for remote roles compared to onsite or hybrid roles?
- Which locations offer the highest average compensation for tech roles?

## 3. Geographic & Work Mode Analysis
- Which cities are the biggest hubs for tech hiring in India?
- What percentage of the tech job market is fully remote vs. hybrid vs. onsite?
- Are certain roles more likely to be remote than others?

## 4. Personal Career Optimization (Skill Gap)
- Given a user's current skill set, what is their match percentage for a specific target role?
- Which missing skills would provide the highest return on investment (i.e., appear in the most job postings for the target role)?

## 5. Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics
- Can we accurately predict the salary of a job based on its title, location, experience requirements, and work mode?
- Which factors (features) have the highest importance in determining a tech worker's salary?
