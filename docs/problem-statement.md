# Problem Statement: JobMarketIQ

## Background
The technology job market is rapidly evolving. With the rise of AI, cloud computing, and remote work, the skills required by employers are shifting continuously. Job seekers often struggle to identify which skills will give them the highest return on investment, while employers face challenges in defining accurate salary bands for emerging roles.

## The Problem
There is a disconnect between the skills job seekers possess and the skills employers demand. Furthermore, compensation in the tech industry is highly opaque and dependent on a complex interplay of role, location, experience, and specific skill combinations.

1. **Skill Gap:** Professionals don't have a data-driven way to compare their current skills against real-time market demands to identify critical learning gaps.
2. **Opaque Compensation:** It is difficult to estimate a fair salary range based on specific combinations of skills, experience, and work modes.
3. **Market Fragmentation:** Job trends vary significantly across geographic regions (e.g., Bangalore vs. Mumbai) and work modes (Remote vs. Hybrid), making it hard to draw high-level insights without centralized data analysis.

## The Solution: JobMarketIQ
JobMarketIQ is an AI-powered intelligence platform that addresses these problems by aggregating and analyzing tech job postings.

### Key Features
- **Skill Demand Analysis:** NLP-based extraction of technical skills from job descriptions to highlight the most in-demand technologies.
- **Salary Intelligence:** Machine Learning (RandomForest) prediction model to estimate salaries based on role, location, experience, and work mode.
- **Personalized Gap Analyzer:** A tool that compares a user's current tech stack against market requirements for a target role, highlighting missing high-value skills.
- **Market Explorer:** Comprehensive dashboards visualizing geographic hotspots, remote work trends, and company hiring volumes.

By providing transparent, data-backed insights, JobMarketIQ empowers professionals to make informed career decisions and upskill effectively for the modern tech landscape.
